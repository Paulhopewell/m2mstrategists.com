The Starting Point

The AI Growth Audit.

A diagnostic session that maps how AI can help your business grow and exit faster. Not a tech audit — a business audit that uses AI as the lever.

What It Is

A Diagnostic, Not a Sales Pitch

Ninety minutes with you and your leadership team. We look at your operations, bottlenecks, growth ambitions and exit timeline — and map where AI can move the needle.

You walk away with a clear, prioritised plan. Not a hundred-page report. Not buzzwords. A practical document that tells you what to do, in what order, and why.

Most owners feel like AI is important but have no idea where to start. The Audit gives you that starting point.

What You Get

Three Deliverables, One Clear Roadmap

1

Pre-Session Brief

A short questionnaire we send before we meet. Twenty minutes of your time, so we spend every minute of the session on substance — not background.

2

90-Min Diagnostic

Held via video call with you and up to two members of your leadership team. We explore your business, bottlenecks and ambitions in depth.

3

Written Roadmap

Delivered within five working days. A clear, prioritised plan showing exactly where AI fits your business — and what to do about it.

The Process

How the Audit Works

Step 01

Where You Are

We assess your current M2M level, real role split, and the gap between what you're doing and what your business needs from you.

Step 02

Where AI Fits

We map specific AI opportunities to your operations — Quick Wins (0–30 days), Foundation (30–90 days), Strategic (3–12 months).

Step 03

How to Get There

A prioritised, ranked plan. Some you can implement next week. Some are longer plays. All chosen for maximum value with the least friction.

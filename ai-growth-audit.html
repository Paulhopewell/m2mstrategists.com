The Growth & AI Audit.

A diagnostic session that maps how M2M Coaching can help your business grow and get you ready to exit on your terms. Not a tech audit — a business audit that uses AI as the lever.

Ninety minutes with you to look at your operations, bottlenecks, growth ambitions and exit timeline. You walk away with a clear, prioritised plan. Not a hundred-page report. Not buzzwords. A practical document that tells you what to do, in what order, and why.

Three Deliverables, One Clear Roadmap

1

Pre-Session Brief

A short questionnaire we send before we meet. Twenty minutes of your time, so we spend every minute of the session on substance — not background.

2

90-Min Diagnostic

Held via video call with you. We explore your business, bottlenecks and ambitions in depth.

3

Written Roadmap

A clear, prioritised plan showing exactly where and how improvements can be made to your business — and what to do about it.

How the Audit Works

Step 01

Where You Are

We assess your current M2M level, real role split, and the gap between what you're doing and what your business needs from you.

Step 02

Where You're Going and How AI Fits

We map growth opportunities and techniques along with specific AI opportunities to your operations.

Step 03

How to Get There

A prioritised, ranked plan, chosen for maximum value with the least friction. Quick Wins (0–30 days), Foundation (30–90 days), Strategic (3–12 months).

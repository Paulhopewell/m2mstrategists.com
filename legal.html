What you send us on a form

We collect personal data when you choose to submit a form. Each form includes a hidden field that must be left empty; it is a spam check, and we do not treat it as a contact detail.

  • Contact (on the contact page). Full name, company, email address, phone number (optional), and a message (optional).
  • Keep in touch (on the M2M Framework page). Full name and email address, so we can send occasional material about the Miner to Millionaire (M2M) Framework.
  • Book enquiry (on the M2M Framework page). Email address, so we can tell you when the Miner to Millionaire book launches.

Where a form submission goes

The forms are Netlify Forms. Netlify stores the submission so we can receive it. Our hosting then runs a function named submission-created, which does three things with the fields you sent:

  • Emails the details to team@m2mstrategists.com.
  • Sends you a short confirmation email from the same address.
  • Creates or updates a contact in HubSpot, using your email address, and, where you provided them, your name, company and phone. It also records which form you used: contact, keep-in-touch, or book-enquiry.

The emails are sent through Resend, which is the email service that function calls. We use the details to reply to you, to send what you asked for, and to keep one record of the enquiry for the team.

If the form cannot be delivered to Netlify, the page opens a draft in your own email program containing what you typed, so you can send it yourself. On the contact page that draft is addressed to ph@m2mstrategists.com. On the framework page it is addressed to team@m2mstrategists.com. Your email provider handles that draft; our form does not.

The website does not pass form details to anyone other than Netlify, Resend, HubSpot, and those team inboxes.
